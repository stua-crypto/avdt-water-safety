Powerboat pre-launch check

Do your pre-launch check on your phone for a RIB or powerboat with an outboard, petrol or electric. Tap each check, note any defects, then save or send your record.

Your club's or centre's procedures come first. Use this if they accept it, or as your own record. It's a general guide to pre-launch checks for small powerboats. Your answers stay on this phone and are never sent to AVDT.

Stay safe while you check

  • Do as much of the check as you can ashore, before you get into the boat.
  • Wear your lifejacket, done up, on the slipway, the pontoon and in the boat, even while you check.
  • Keep low and keep a hand for yourself. Slipways, pontoons and tubes are slippery, and it's easy to trip or fall overboard.
  • Don't lean out over the transom or the side to look at the engine. Tilt it up, or check it from ashore.
  • Keep your phone in a waterproof pouch, on a lanyard or clipped to you, so it can't go over the side. Put it away before you cast off.
  • Never use your phone while you're helming.

2. Checks

Tap OK or Defect for each one. For a defect, say what's wrong and add up to 3 photos. Checks that don't always apply also have N/A.

3. Your record

Sending photos? "Send or share" sends the words only. To send your defect photos too, use Save as PDF and send the PDF, or take the photos again with the camera in WhatsApp after you send the message.

"Send or share" opens your phone's share menu, so you can send the record as a message by WhatsApp, text or email. "Save as PDF" makes a PDF copy, including any defect photos, that you can save to Files or send. On a computer, the record is copied for you to paste and the PDF downloads.

Keep it on your phone

Add this check to your home screen and it opens like an app, with its own icon.

  • iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open this page in Chrome, tap the three dots at the top right, then Add to home screen.

You need a signal to open it. Once it's open, the checks work without signal and your answers are saved on the phone as you go. Clubs and centres can print a waterproof QR sticker for each boat with the free boat check sticker maker.

Found a dangerous defect? Don't launch. Tell the person in charge of the boats straight away, and label the boat so nobody else takes it out. If something goes wrong afloat, call the Coastguard on VHF channel 16, or dial 999 and ask for the Coastguard.
