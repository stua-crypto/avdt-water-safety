Boat check sticker and card maker

Make a QR sticker or card for each boat. Helms scan it on the console or the engine key and the pre-launch check opens with that boat already filled in.

1. Your boats

One boat per line: the boat's name or number, then petrol or electric. For example: Rescue 4, petrol. Anything left out is set to petrol.

2. Sticker options

Console stickers for each boat
Key tag stickers for each boat (for the engine key)
Boat cards (credit card size)

Use "A4 sheet to cut out" on a normal printer. "For a card printer" is only for printers that print plastic cards: on a normal printer each card fills a whole A4 page.

Console stickers are 50 mm square, 15 to an A4 sheet: put one on the console or inside the boat where the helm sees it, out of direct spray. Key tag stickers are 30 mm square, 40 to a sheet, and carry the same QR code and boat name, so they stay with the engine key. Each sticker has a thin grey cutting line.

3. Check and print

Scan a sticker below with your phone camera to test it before you print.

Boat card, front and back

Boat cards are 85.6 × 54 mm, the size of a bank card, with the QR code on the front and the check steps on the back. "A4 sheet to cut out" puts 8 cards on a page with the backs on the next page, lined up for double-sided printing. "For a card printer" makes one card per page, front then back. Choose double-sided in your card printer's settings. Laminate them, or keep them in the boat store, the kit bag or a waterproof card holder.

Print at 100% size ("actual size"), not "fit to page". Print on plain paper and cut out, or on sticker label sheets. Do a test print on plain paper first and hold it against the label sheet to check the stickers line up. On a boat, print on waterproof vinyl labels or laminate the stickers, or the sea will soon finish them off.

Everything happens in your browser. Your boat list is kept on this device so you can come back to it, and is never sent to AVDT. The stickers open the free powerboat pre-launch check.
